Website Conversion Audit: 10 Quick Wins to Get More Leads This Month

Shaheer Shaikh Jul 6, 20268 min readConversion

Before spending more on ads or SEO, look at what happens to the traffic you already have. If 100 people visit and only one contacts you, doubling the conversion rate is the same as doubling your traffic — and usually much cheaper. Here are ten quick wins I check on every site.

  1. Clear headline above the fold. Say what you do, who it is for and where you work in one line.
  2. One primary call to action. Make the main button stand out in colour and repeat it down the page.
  3. Click-to-call on mobile. Your phone number should be a tappable link in the header, not an image.
  4. Shorter forms. Remove every field you do not need for the first conversation.
  5. Trust signals near forms. Reviews, ratings, guarantees and certifications reduce hesitation.
  6. Faster load time. Compress images, use WebP, remove unused plugins and enable caching.
  7. Real photos. Replace generic stock images with your team, your work and your results.
  8. Answer objections. Add a short FAQ covering price, timing, area and process.
  9. Instant follow-up. Make sure every form submission triggers an immediate text or email.
  10. Track everything. Set up analytics goals so you can see which pages and buttons convert.

How to run your own audit

Open your website on your phone and pretend you are a customer in a hurry. Can you tell what the business does in five seconds? Can you contact them in two taps? Does the page load before you get bored? Note every moment of friction — each one is a leak.

Then watch real behaviour. Tools like heatmaps and session recordings show where people click, how far they scroll and where they give up. You will often find that visitors never even reach your form.

Prioritise by effort and impact

Fix the quick, high-impact items first: phone link, headline, button, form length and speed. These usually take a day and show results within weeks. Bigger changes like new landing pages or a redesign come next, guided by what the data tells you.

Conversion optimisation is not a one-time project. Small improvements every month compound into a website that keeps getting better.
