What services do you offer?

WordPress websites, GoHighLevel CRM and automation, sales funnels, AI agents and chatbots, Shopify stores, and complete lead generation systems that connect all of them.

How much does a website cost?

Prices are one-time project fees in USD: WordPress websites start at $150, Shopify stores at $250 and GoHighLevel funnels at $200. Larger builds and custom work are quoted within 24 hours.

How long does a project take?

A GHL funnel takes 3–5 days, a WordPress starter site 5–7 days, a business website or CRM setup 1–2 weeks, and a full store or automation suite 2–4 weeks.

Which platform is right for my business?

Shopify if you mainly sell products, WordPress if you need content and SEO, and GoHighLevel if your business runs on leads, bookings and follow-up. Many businesses combine WordPress with GoHighLevel.

Do you work with clients outside Pakistan?

Yes. I work remotely with businesses in the US, UK, Australia, Dubai and Pakistan.

Can you add AI or automation to my existing website?

Usually, yes — chat agents, instant SMS/email follow-up and online booking can be connected to most existing websites through GoHighLevel.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. Monthly maintenance and support plans cover updates, backups, security and speed checks. Premium packages include 30 days of post-launch support.

How do we get started?

Send a message through the contact form or WhatsApp with what you're building. You'll get a recommendation and a quote within 24 hours.
