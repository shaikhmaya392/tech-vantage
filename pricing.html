Are these prices one-time or monthly?

All project prices are one-time fees in USD with no hidden costs. Third-party subscriptions you use — like hosting, a domain, a Shopify plan or a GoHighLevel account — are separate from the project fee.

Do you offer maintenance after launch?

Yes — monthly maintenance and support plans are available for updates, backups, security, speed checks and small content changes.

How fast will my project be delivered?

Each package lists its delivery time — from 3–5 days for a single funnel to 2–4 weeks for a complete automation suite.

Which platform should I choose?

Shopify if you mainly sell products, WordPress if you need content and SEO, GoHighLevel if you live on leads and appointments. Tell me your goals and I'll recommend the right fit.
