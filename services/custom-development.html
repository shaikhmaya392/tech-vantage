Questions about Custom Web

Can't find your answer? Ask the assistant in English or Roman Urdu, or message on WhatsApp.

When should I choose custom code over WordPress or GHL?

When you need something a platform can't do well — a unique workflow, a custom dashboard or CRM, an interactive tool, or maximum speed without plugins.

What technologies do you use?

HTML, CSS and JavaScript for the front end, PHP where a server is needed, and APIs to connect the services you already use.

Can I see examples?

Yes — the projects below are hand-coded demos you can open and try: 4 complete websites (real estate, restaurant, gym and creative agency) and 4 web apps (a CRM, an analytics dashboard, a booking system and a solar quote funnel).

How is custom work priced?

Every custom build is quoted after a short call about your requirements, so you only pay for what you need.
